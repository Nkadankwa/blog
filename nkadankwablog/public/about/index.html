Building a little more confidence with every project

I’m Nkadankwa, a curious builder documenting my journey through self-hosting, home labs, Linux, automation, and the occasional spectacularly educational mistake.

This is where I turn experiments into notes I can revisit—and hopefully useful starting points for someone else. I prefer learning by building: trying the thing, untangling what breaks, and sharing the lessons rather than pretending it was smooth from the start.

What I’m exploring

  • Home labs and self-hosted services
  • Linux, Docker, and practical infrastructure
  • Local AI, automation, and open-source tools
  • The systems and habits that make learning sustainable

Why this blog exists

Most technical journeys look tidy only in retrospect. This blog keeps the real version: the research, false starts, wins, and changes of mind along the way. If a post helps you avoid one of my mistakes or gives you a nudge to try a project, it has done its job.

You can also find my projects and code on GitHub.

Build. Learn. Improve. Repeat.

A personal blog about home labs, self-hosting, and figuring things out one project at a time.
