When Research Meets Reality: My Unexpected Home Lab Setup?

July 21, 2025◷ 1 min readUpdated Jul 21, 2025
On this page
  • A better surprise
  • What I learned

I began looking for homelab hardware convinced I wanted a Raspberry Pi. I had used one at school, liked the size, and assumed it was the natural place to start.

Then I did the maths. A newer Pi was already stretching my budget, and the power supply, storage, enclosure, cooling, and other extras made the total less appealing. I compared SBCs and mini PCs and learned that the “cheap” option depends on the whole setup—not just the headline price.

A better surprise

The answer arrived from somewhere I had not planned for: an old laptop a friend was ready to let go. I bought it for less than the alternatives, replaced the battery, fitted an SSD I already owned, and updated the system. It was not the tiny, purpose-built server I had pictured. It was a real machine I could start using.

What I learned

  • Familiar hardware is not automatically the best hardware.
  • Accessories and upgrade paths belong in the budget.
  • Used equipment can be an excellent first lab when it gives you room to learn.

The project became more practical the moment I stopped chasing the ideal device. Next up: choosing an operating system and seeing what this unexpected server can teach me.
