## Check your copy

The [SHA-256 fingerprints](/blog/ebooks/v1.1/SHA256SUMS.txt) let you compare your download with these published files. In PowerShell, run `Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256` followed by the path to your downloaded PDF, and compare the resulting hash with the matching entry.

A matching fingerprint means the files have the same contents. These PDFs are unsigned; a checksum is not a certificate-based identity check or protection against copying.
