## Three places I return to when I am stuck

Some websites are useful because they give a quick answer. Others are useful because they help me learn how to find the next answer myself. These are three I keep nearby.

### LabEx

LabEx offers hands-on technical labs. I like the idea of learning in an environment where a mistake is part of the exercise rather than a reason to worry about breaking my own setup.

### CS50

CS50 is a reminder that a well-explained foundation stays useful long after the first lesson. It is especially good for stepping back from a specific bug and reconnecting with the underlying programming idea.

### VisuAlgo

VisuAlgo makes algorithms easier to picture. Seeing a structure change step by step is helpful when code alone feels too abstract.

I have not completed every path these sites offer. What I value is that each gives me a different way to learn: practise, understand, and visualise.
