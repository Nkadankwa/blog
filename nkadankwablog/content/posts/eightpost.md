I wanted a note-taking system that could grow with me: cross-platform, capable of syncing, comfortable with Markdown, and flexible enough for mind maps and project notes.

That led me through Simplenote, Obsidian, Evernote, Joplin, Notion, and Zoho Notebook. I compared them through the things that mattered to me: cost, offline access, privacy, plugins, syncing, and whether the tool could eventually live in a self-hosted setup.

## Choosing for the long term

The shortlist became Obsidian and Joplin. Obsidian has a strong ecosystem, but Joplin felt closer to the system I wanted to build: open source, Markdown-first, self-hostable, and able to sync through services such as Nextcloud.

Joplin is not the perfect answer. Plugins add capability, but also add setup and maintenance. Still, that trade-off felt worthwhile because I value control over a polished but closed workflow.

My next step is to make it practical: test sync, try the mind-map workflow, and build a backup habit before I depend on it. A notes app is only useful if I trust it with the ideas I want to keep.
