To be honest, I didn’t expect to face so many challenges when setting up
my home lab. But as we all know, life doesn’t always go our way. That’s
when I decided the best way to stay motivated was to document my
journey—and that’s how this blog was born.

## **Where It Started**

The home lab journey began as just a thought sparked by a simple
question:  
>**“I have so many diverse interests—how can I integrate them, or at least keep them all in one place?”**

I pondered this for a while, but eventually forgot about it. Then one
day, while doomscrolling on TikTok, I came across a video about Linux.
The creator explained how he manages his passwords and why he has
multiple steps for securing the documents on his home server. I ended up
following his account.

Later, that original question came back to me—and so did the video.
That’s when the idea hit:  
>**Why don’t I start a home server?**

And down the rabbit hole I went. As I began researching home servers, I
discovered that what I actually wanted was something broader—a **home
lab**. While the terms are often used interchangeably, the scope of a
home lab felt more fitting for my goals.

## **Starting a Home Lab**

I started researching what I’d need to begin. I joined communities,
watched tutorials, and gradually came up with a list of questions I
needed to answer:

1.  What is the goal of my home lab? (Though I already had an answer.)

2.  What kind of hardware will I use for the home lab?

3.  Will I have the budget to make it happen?

4.  What operating systems should I use?

5.  What skills or knowledge do I need to build and manage it?

6.  What safety or security protocols should I implement?

I haven’t thought much past the sixth question—but I’ll cross that
bridge when I get there.

## **Research**

After gathering advice from various platforms, I used **Google Gemini’s
deep research tool** to generate a detailed report comparing SBCs
(Single-Board Computers) and mini PCs that could match or exceed the
Raspberry Pi based on my needs. The result? A **50-page report** that
helped me narrow my options.

## **Lessons Learned So Far**

From just the beginning, I’ve already learned a few key things:

> **Planning** – The importance of defining your goals before diving in.
>
> **Budgeting** – Always consider your limits and work within them.

## **What’s Next?**

Not much has been built yet, but I’m excited to keep going and share my
progress as I learn. If you’re just starting your home lab journey too,
stick around—let’s build and learn together.

## **Final Thoughts**

If you're thinking about starting a home lab, just take that first step.
Don’t wait for the “perfect” setup. Begin small, learn as you go, and
remember—you’re not alone on this journey.

## **Farewell**

After all the research and planning, I was finally ready to start
choosing the actual hardware—whether SBCs or mini PCs would best suit my
needs. I went deep into comparisons, specs, and future-proofing. But as
you’ll find out in the next post… sometimes things don’t go as
